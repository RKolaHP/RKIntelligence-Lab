
# AutoPulse AI

> Intelligent Automotive Data & Predictive Maintenance Platform

## 1. Product Overview

AutoPulse AI is an automotive intelligence application designed to transform vehicle telemetry, maintenance records, operational data, and asset information into actionable insights.

The platform aims to help fleet operators, automotive businesses, maintenance teams, and vehicle owners monitor vehicle health, understand operational performance, and anticipate maintenance requirements.

## 2. Product Vision

Build an intelligent automotive platform that connects vehicle data, operational analytics, and predictive intelligence through a unified application experience.
