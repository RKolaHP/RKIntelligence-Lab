
# EduSphere Intelligence

> Academic Analytics & Research Intelligence Platform

## 1. Product Overview

EduSphere Intelligence is an education and research analytics application designed to bring academic information, institutional reporting, student performance indicators, and research data into a unified analytical environment.

The platform will help educational institutions and researchers transform disconnected information into structured insights.

## 2. Product Vision

Create an intelligent education ecosystem that improves academic visibility, institutional decision-making, and research information management.
