
# DermaSense AI

> AI-Powered Skincare & Ingredient Intelligence Platform

## 1. Product Overview

DermaSense AI is a beauty and skincare intelligence application designed to help users understand skincare products, explore ingredients, extract product-label information, and compare formulations.

The platform will combine product information, document processing, structured ingredient knowledge, and carefully scoped AI-assisted insights.

## 2. Product Vision

Build an intelligent skincare discovery platform that makes product information more understandable, transparent, and accessible.
